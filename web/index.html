PARTICULATE DYNAMICS // ARID ECONOMICS
Q1: Why dry split-microfiber static shear over pressurized wet washing?
In arid high-irradiance solar corridors (such as Rajasthan and the Middle East), industrial water costs exceed ₹0.80 per liter and requires continuous replenishment trucks. More critically, washing sun-baked solar panels operating at 65°C to 75°C with ambient water induces extreme thermal shock, triggering microscopic solar cell fracture and delamination. Furthermore, residual mineral evaporation forms an alkaline crust that bakes onto antireflective coatings. A.R.C.’s 800 GSM plush split-microfiber sleeve rotates at 1.05 m/s tip velocity with an electrostatic surface charge that lifts silica particulates under zero moisture and zero thermal strain.
KINEMATIC CONSTRAINT // DEGREE OF FREEDOM
Q2: Why a 3-wheel pinching carriage instead of a traditional 4-wheel box carriage?
Classical 4-wheel linear carriages are over-constrained. When mounting two parallel guide rails separated by 340 mm across standard extruded aluminum profiles, minor manufacturing tolerances (±0.25 mm) and non-uniform thermal expansion cause 4-wheel carriages to mechanically bind and stall micro gearmotors. Project A.R.C. uses an exact kinematic 3-wheel configuration: two fixed upper Delrin Mini V-wheels establish the primary reference plane, while a single lower wheel mounted on a ±0.75 mm eccentric brass cam provides adjustable normal pinching force. This ensures complete kinematic determinism without over-constraint.
MECHANICAL GEOMETRY // ANTI-RACKING RATIO
Q3: How does the 110 mm carriage wheelbase mathematically prevent gantry racking?
Gantry skew and racking occur when the moment arm created by traction differentials exceeds the carriage guiding length. The classic mechanical engineering threshold to eliminate racking without synchronized ball screws is W / L > 0.25 (wheelbase W divided by crossbeam span L). Project A.R.C. features an engineered wheelbase of W = 110 mm across a crossbeam span of L = 340 mm, yielding:
W / L = 110 mm / 340 mm = 0.324 > 0.250 (Anti-Racking Threshold)
This 29.6% safety margin above the racking threshold guarantees that the gantry cannot cock diagonally or wedge into the V-slot channels, even under asymmetric roller dusting resistance.
MATERIALS SCIENCE // THERMAL GLASS TRANSITION
Q4: Why obsidian PETG instead of PLA or ABS for structural 3D-printed brackets?
Standard PLA has a glass transition temperature (Tg) of only 55°C–60°C. Field measurements on active monocrystalline solar panels in summer routinely exceed 65°C, causing PLA motor mounts and endplates to undergo ductile creep, losing bearing alignment and loosening belt/wheel tensions. ABS offers high thermal resistance (Tg ≈ 105°C), but exhibits massive anisotropic shrinkage and layer delamination during non-enclosed printing. PETG (Tg = 80°C) delivers ideal thermal headroom, exceptional inter-layer shear strength, and inherent UV resistance against sunlight degradation.
MECHATRONICS // TORQUE & ENERGY BUDGET
Q5: What are the exact torque sizing margins for the traverse and roller motors?
Traverse locomotion uses dual N20 12V 300 RPM micro metal gearmotors yielding 0.28 N·m continuous torque at the drive wheels. At a 15° solar panel tilt, gravitational downhill resistance and rolling friction total 0.038 N·m, providing an enormous 14.6× torque margin:
τavail / τreq = 0.280 N·m / 0.0192 N·m = 14.58× Margin (15° Incline)
The cleaning roller is powered by a high-torque 25GA-370 motor delivering 0.42 N·m against an estimated microfiber pile shear drag of 0.026 N·m, yielding a 15.8× margin. Power is supplied by a 2S 18650 Li-ion pack (5200 mAh), which provides 53.4 single-panel cleaning passes on a single charge.
AVIONICS // INRUSH MITIGATION & SOFT-START
Q6: How does the firmware prevent brownout resets during simultaneous motor start?
Simultaneous direct step energization of the 25GA-370 roller motor and dual N20 traverse motors produces a combined stall inrush current spike of 4.80 A. Across internal battery ESR and wiring impedance, this voltage sag trips the ESP32’s internal brownout detector (2.80 V reset). Our firmware executes a 350 ms quadratic PWM soft-start algorithm:
D(t) = Dmax · ( t / Tramp )2  |  Peak Inrush: 1.08 A (Nominal)
This smooth acceleration limits instantaneous inductive inrush to just 1.08 A, leaving a stable 7.36 V bus with zero microcontroller resets.
METROLOGY & CALIBRATION // 0.00 MM DATUM
Q7: How is the physical solar surface locked to the Z = 0.00 mm datum plane?
The desktop testbed utilizes four 40 mm elevated PETG riser feet that isolate the aluminum extrusions from uneven work surfaces. Precision alignment brackets clamp the 3.2 mm float glass solar panel directly to the 2020 extrusion V-slots, locking the top glass surface exactly at Z = 0.00 mm. Dual 72.03 mm anti-sag pedestals support the glass span center, limiting panel deflection to under 0.05 mm under the calibrated 1.20 mm roller microfiber compression.
